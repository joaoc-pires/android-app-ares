City approves new tram line

By Daniel Costa · 16 November 2026

The city council voted on Monday to approve a new tram line connecting the riverside district with the university campus, ending years of debate about how to ease congestion on the northern ring road.

Advertisement

Construction is expected to begin next spring and to take roughly three years. The line will have eleven stops and is planned to carry around forty thousand passengers a day once it is fully operational.

Supporters say the project will cut journey times and reduce air pollution, while some local businesses worry about disruption during the works. The council has promised a compensation scheme for shops along the route.

A public consultation on the exact location of the stops will open next month, and residents will be able to comment online or at a series of events in community centres along the planned route.
